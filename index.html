About Magpie Corporation.

Magpie Corporation a syndicate of talents is poised to provide turnkey solutions in the areas of Hospital Marketing, Informatics & Media planning and organizing of Seminar & Exposition. Each domain is catered in alliance with experts of their respective field. Informatics & Media Division handles the publication of Proceedings, Abstract Books, Souvenirs, Brochure and all types of offset, digital printing. Video Abstracting is also undertaken by the division. Besides, the division is actively working with many Societies, Institutions and NGO's for Print & Media Management and PR services. Seminar & Exposition Division with its fleet of professionals aims to serve the client with a “beehive approach”. The division is striving to carve the concept of Event management in organizing Seminars, Conventions and Continued Medical Education (CME's) Programs. The division handles an event on total turnkey basis and provides the client a Single Window Service from concept to creation. Cultivation of concept of Theme Seminars is also a pioneering attempt done by the division.
